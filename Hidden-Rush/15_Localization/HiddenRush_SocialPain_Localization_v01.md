# Localization Notes — Social Pain (v01)

Idioms / references to adapt when dubbing or subtitling into other languages:

| Phrase | Issue | Suggested handling |
|---|---|---|
| "left on read" | read-receipts less universal; wording varies | localize to the native phrase for "seen, no reply" |
| "still screaming at the smell of toast" | toast = Western breakfast; smoke-detector-vs-toast is US/UK trope | keep smoke-detector metaphor, swap "toast" for a locally common harmless smoke (frying, incense) |
| "group chat" / "added to the plan" | fine in most markets | verify platform-neutral wording |
| "Tylenol" | US brand | say "acetaminophen/paracetamol" in dub; keep brand only in EN |
| "three dots" (typing indicator) | universal on major apps | OK |

Dub targets to prioritize (by psychology-audience size): ES, PT-BR, DE, HI, FR, AR.
Keep the emphasis-word list (physical, borrowed, false alarm, not a flaw, name) mapped per language.
