# Hidden Rush — Music & Sound-Design Plan

**Video:** *Being Ignored Isn't Emotional. It's Physical.* · v01

Principle: **music serves the voice, never competes.** No constant trailer-epic. Silence is a tool —
use it at the two designed drop-beats (0:07 area and the 6:05 midpoint). Duck music **-14 to -18 dB**
under narration; let it rise only in the wordless beats.

## Signature SFX list
- Notification blip (cold open), typing-dots stop tick, cursor blink, heartbeat (recurring — ties
  emotional beats), scanner hum, ball-pass "tick", resonant low hit on brain-node reveal, glitch on
  "just ignore it," smoke-alarm chirp, pill "tap," warm breath at climax.
- **Heartbeat is the through-line:** appears at 0:12, disappears at the midpoint drop, returns
  *steadier* at climax (#32) — an audible version of the story's arc.
